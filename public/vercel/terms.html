OpenSI Vercel Integration Terms

Effective October 11, 2026.

By connecting your Vercel account, you authorize OpenSI to perform project creation, file uploads, deployments, and integration-owned environment variable updates when you request those actions through OpenSI. You are responsible for selecting the correct account, project files, and deployment target and for complying with Vercel's terms and your Vercel plan.

OpenSI shows deployment results received from Vercel. A successful build does not guarantee that every application feature works. Vercel controls hosting availability, billing, and account limits. You may disconnect OpenSI in its connections page and revoke access in your Vercel account.

For assistance, see support. The privacy notice describes the information handled by this integration.
