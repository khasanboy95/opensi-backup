OpenSI Vercel Integration Privacy

Effective October 11, 2026.

When you connect Vercel, OpenSI receives an OAuth access token, installation identifier, and the selected team identifier. OpenSI associates this connection with your OpenSI user account and encrypts the token on its server. Other OpenSI users cannot use or view your connection.

The requested Vercel permissions cover projects, deployments, and integration-owned project environment variables on the Vercel account you select. OpenSI uses the projects permission to check the connection and create an OpenSI project on Vercel. When you request a deployment, OpenSI uploads that project's source files and server environment values that you linked to the deployment. OpenSI reads build state, logs, and the deployment URL to show them in your workspace. Vercel processes this information under its own privacy terms.

OpenSI stores the encrypted access token until you disconnect the connection. Disconnecting disables the token in OpenSI, while historical deployment records may remain in your workspace. You can also revoke the integration in Vercel. The integration does not request billing or domain-management permissions.

For questions or deletion requests, use the contact address shown on the support page.
